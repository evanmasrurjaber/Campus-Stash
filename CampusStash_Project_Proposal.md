## CampusStash
The project aims to build a dual purpose website that serves as a digital platform for reporting and finding lost items and a shared marketplace where students can buy and sell items. The website exclusively targets only the University students to make it a safe place for exchanging goods whether it's a lost item or an item for sale.

The title "Campus Stash" was chosen because it indicates the idea of a personal inventory (Stash) and it is only exclusive for the students of the University (Campus). It represents a secure and exclusive platform where valuable items will be traded among peers.

## Project Rationale

### Background
Students often lose valuable items and the current lost & found system is completely manual which makes it harder for students to find their lost items. They have to go through manual entries on the lost & found desk to see if their lost items are possibly submitted there and if it's not there they have to repeat the process again another time until they lose hope. Similarly, students find it hard to find out which sellers are trustable in open marketplaces to buy goods and the student entrepreneurs cannot find proper platforms to promote their goods.

Hence, this project has the opportunity to digitize and unify the services mentioned in a single platform with a high trust environment by forcing authentication of every user via university email address. The project meets the need for a digital lost & found and a student only buying and selling platform of the students as they will be able to get these services effortlessly which was previously dependent on scattered social media group posts for notifying that an item was found on campus and promoting goods offered by student entrepreneurs. The point of doing the project is saving time and money for the students when buying items from the marketplace and to reduce the stress and hassle of finding lost items.

### Scope
The project will try to reach the active student body and faculty of a specific college or university as the audience.
* Students who have lost or found items, as well as students looking to affordably buy or profitably sell used academic materials.
* It will save users money on academic gear, reduce the stress of losing valuables, and provide a secure, scam-free environment to coordinate meetups with peers.

The scope spans the entire student population of the target university.

## Project Objectives
To address the issues effectively, the following objectives need to be fulfilled:
* To easily search lost items through names, keywords and categories
* To notify students when a similar item the student is searching is found
* To create an easy to navigate p2p marketplace for students
* To create a trustable buying and selling platform by verifying identity of the students through university email

## Project Approach
Mern stack will be used to build the website. Potential risks include low initial user adoption, moderation of inappropriate marketplace listings, and the technical challenge of ensuring strict email validation to keep non-university individuals out.

## Conclusion
The CampusStash proposes a modern and centralized solution to two main key problems students face by building a digitized version of lost & found and a buying and selling market place. The website will feature an identity verification system that will stop outsiders from entering the platform and keep the platform scam free. Overall, this project will help to minimize student time waste and stress significantly by providing these features to the students.
